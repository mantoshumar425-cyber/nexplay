1. Overview

NEXTPLAY is a browser-based gaming portal. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be handled when you visit the site, play embedded games, or use features such as favourites, player profiles, rewards, and preferences.

Use of the website means you have read this policy. If you do not agree with it, please stop using the website.

2. Information stored by NEXTPLAY

The current website may save game preferences and progress directly in your browser using local storage or similar browser storage. This can include a gamer name or Player ID you enter, avatar choice, XP and coins, favourites, recently opened games, theme and sound settings, daily rewards, and locally saved chat or activity entries.

This browser-stored information is generally kept on the device and browser you use. It may be removed if you clear site data, use a different browser/device, or reset your progress. The current local-only features do not automatically make your profile or local chat visible to other visitors.

3. Information sent to third parties

NEXTPLAY may request game lists, thumbnails, and game content from GamePix or other third-party providers. When you open an embedded game, that provider may receive technical information such as your IP address, browser/device details, and information needed to deliver the content. The provider's own privacy policy and terms apply to its services.

If third-party advertising, analytics, social sharing, or other external services are enabled now or later, those providers may use cookies, device identifiers, or similar technologies under their own policies. Review the relevant provider's notices before using those services.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

The website may use browser storage to remember settings and progress. Third-party games or services may use their own cookies or similar technologies. You can manage or clear browser storage and cookies in your browser settings; doing so may reset locally saved progress or affect game functionality.

5. How information is used

  • To provide game browsing and gameplay features.
  • To remember local preferences and progress.
  • To protect the site, diagnose technical problems, and prevent misuse.
  • To support third-party games or services you choose to open.

NEXTPLAY should not request sensitive personal information for ordinary gameplay. Do not enter passwords, financial information, government identifiers, or private information into public profile names or chat fields.

6. Children and younger users

NEXTPLAY is a general gaming website. Children should use the site with appropriate parent or guardian guidance. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information to the site should contact the site operator using the contact details published on the website so the concern can be reviewed.

7. Data security and retention

Browser-stored data remains on your device unless a feature or third-party provider sends information elsewhere. No website or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. Third-party providers control their own retention and security practices. You can delete local progress using browser site-data controls.
